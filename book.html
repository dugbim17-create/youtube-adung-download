Why ElevenLabs is the Core Engine of Adung Studio Book

Long-form audiobook storytelling demands emotional tension, believable pacing, and distinct character identities that conventional local TTS engines cannot achieve.

🎙️ 1. Multi-Character Voice Casting Matrix

Authors assign persistent ElevenLabs Voice IDs across entire manuscript chapters. Protagonists, narrators, and secondary characters maintain identical vocal characteristics across 20+ hours of continuous audio.

⏱️ 2. Natural Pacing & Micro-Breath Punctuation

Automated text pre-processing balances sentence rhythm with micro-pauses (180ms comma breaths, 680ms paragraph pauses) avoiding the synthetic rush of generic speech generation.

🎭 3. Dynamic Emotion Modulation

Seamlessly switches tone from solemn historical exposition to rapid dialogue tension, whispers, and climactic revelations utilizing ElevenLabs Multilingual V3 prompting cues.

🎧 4. Broadcast & ACX Master Export

Built-in RMS loudness normalization (-23 dB to -18 dB), noise floor verification (< -60 dB), and chapter ID3 tag generation compliant with Audible and Spotify requirements.

From Raw Manuscript to Mastered Audiobook in 4 Steps

Designed for writers, creators, and audio producers who value speed, quality, and complete creative control.

📄

01. Ingest & Tokenize

Import long-form manuscripts in Markdown, TXT, or DOCX. The parser automatically delineates dialogue tags from descriptive narrator prose.

👥

02. Cast Character Voices

Assign distinct ElevenLabs voice profiles to each character with granular controls over stability, emotional exaggeration, and vocal style.

🎛️

03. Pacing & Sound Design

Tune pauses, insert subtle foley (turning pages, background room tones, dramatic stings), and preview audio in real time on the timeline.

📦

04. ACX-Compliant Export

Batch render finished chapters into broadcast-ready WAV and MP3 with synchronized subtitle files (.SRT / .VTT) and embedded chapter art.
